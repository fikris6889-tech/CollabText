CollabText

Real-time collaborative plain text, synced with a hand-built RGA CRDT over a hand-built WebSocket server. No sync libraries involved.

Tip: open /?doc=demo in two browser tabs (or two browsers) side by side and type in both at once. Every keystroke becomes one CRDT operation, sent over a WebSocket connection whose handshake and framing are implemented from scratch in src/wsServer.js — see the README for the write-up.
